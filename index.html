LeaveNote

Stop coding when you want. Paste your changes, and a local AI writes the handover note that helps tomorrow-you start where you left off.

The AI runs on your device using Qwen2.5 (open-weight) and WebLLM. First load downloads model files (hundreds of MB). Requires WebGPU; devices without it get an honest message, not a fake demo. No API key, no account, no server inference.

Privacy & limits

Your diff and notes are processed by a model running in this browser, not an AI service. The app has no server, no analytics and no uploads. Hosting/CDN/model providers receive ordinary file requests and your IP address. Model files may stay in the browser cache; saved notes live in this browser's local storage until you clear them.


A small model can misread a diff or invent a next step. The note is a memory aid, not ground truth - review it, and keep your real source of truth in git. Long diffs are trimmed to the first 1600 characters, so paste the part tomorrow-you most needs.





Open-weight model: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (Apache 2.0). Inference: WebLLM (Apache 2.0). Built for Hacktoberfest 2026, DEV Open-Source AI Challenge, Week 3: Slow and Steady.
