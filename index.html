LeaveNote

Stop coding when you want. Paste your changes, and a local AI writes the handover note that gets tomorrow-you back up to speed - so future you can be a little lazier.

The AI runs on your device using Qwen2.5 (open-weight) and WebLLM. First load downloads the model files (a few hundred MB), once, then they are cached. Requires WebGPU; devices without it get an honest message, not a fake demo. No API key, no account, no server inference.

How it works (and why you can trust the note)

Plain code reads your diff first: file names, added and removed line counts, leftover TODOs and debug lines. The UNFINISHED and WATCH OUT sections always come from that direct reading, never from the AI. The local AI only writes the two lines it is good at: a plain-English summary of what changed and a suggested first step for tomorrow - from facts it is handed, so it cannot invent work you did not do.


Every section of the note is labelled: written by the on-device AI, or parsed straight from the diff text. If the AI draft fails the app's format checks, that section falls back to the parsed version and says so. No fake AI output, ever.






Privacy & limits

Your diff and notes are processed by a model running in this browser, not an AI service. The app has no server, no analytics and no uploads. Hosting/CDN/model providers receive ordinary file requests and your IP address. Model files may stay in the browser cache; saved notes live in this browser's local storage until you clear them.


A small model can still misphrase a sentence. The note is a memory aid, not ground truth - review it, and keep your real source of truth in git. Long diffs are trimmed to the first 1600 characters, so paste the part tomorrow-you most needs.





Open-weight model: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (Apache 2.0). Inference: WebLLM (Apache 2.0). Built for Hacktoberfest 2026, DEV Open-Source AI Challenge, Week 3: Slow and Steady.
